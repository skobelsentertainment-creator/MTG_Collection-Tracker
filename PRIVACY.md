# Privacy Policy for MTG Collection Tracker

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

MTG Collection Tracker is developed and published by Skobel Software.

## Information We Collect

Skobel Software does not collect, transmit, sell or share users' personal information or collection data.

The application does not require a user account and does not contain advertising or analytics tracking.

## Locally Stored Data

Card collection information, purchase prices, acquisition dates, storage locations, preferences, cached card images, backups and CSV files are stored locally on the user's computer or in a location selected by the user.

If a user selects a folder managed by a third-party cloud-storage service, that service may synchronize the files according to its own terms and privacy policy.

## Internet Access

The application connects to:

- Scryfall to retrieve Magic: The Gathering set, card, image and pricing information.
- GitHub to check whether a newer version of MTG Collection Tracker is available.

These third-party services may receive standard network information, such as the user's IP address, when the application connects to them. Their own privacy policies govern their handling of that information.

## Data Control

Users control the information stored by the application. Collection records can be edited or deleted within the application. Users may also delete locally created databases, backups, exported CSV files and cached card images from their computer.

Uninstalling the application may not automatically remove user-created collection databases or backup files, allowing other users to preserve their collection between installations.

## Children's Privacy

MTG Collection Tracker does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published in this repository.
